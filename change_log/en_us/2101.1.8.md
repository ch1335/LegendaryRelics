# Changelog

## 2101.1.8

New Items

- **Magic Breaker** (integration with Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks): A one-handed sword built from the Spellbreaker. It comes with Counterspell I and grants +15% spell cooldown reduction and +15% spell resistance. Hits inflict **Magic Suppression** (the target deals 30% less magic damage for 5 seconds) and **Ender Suppression** (the target cannot teleport for 3 seconds).
- **Soul Armor Set** (integration with Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks): Soul Wizard Hat, Soul Robe, Soul Leggings and Soul Boots. Each piece grants +2 max health, +125 max mana and +5% spell power; the boots add +10% movement speed; the robe has 1 spell slot.
  - Set bonus **Soul**: dealing spell damage builds stacks (up to 10). Each stack grants spell power and spell cooldown reduction. At 10 stacks, spell hits fire 3 soul missiles that also strike up to 2 creatures within 5 blocks of the main target. Stacks decay over time.
- **Holy Cloak**: Adds a new "Cloak" curio slot. The cloak flutters naturally as you move. Effect **Holy Barrier**: completely negates the damage you take and grants 1 second of invincibility, with a 10 second cooldown.
- **Thunderbolt Necklace**: A new curio that grants Chain Lightning. Dropped by Elder Guardians.

Balance

- New attribute **Magic Find**: rare loot that does not drop gets one extra independent retry, raising its final chance to base chance x (1 + Magic Find) **without affecting the drop chance of anything else**. It starts at 0, so it only takes effect once an item or effect grants it
- New attribute **Seeking**: rare mobs whose spawn weight is below 2% of the whole spawn list get their weight raised to base weight x (1 + Seeking), using the highest Seeking among players within 128 blocks, uncapped. Only affects natural spawning; mob spawners and mobs placed by structures are unaffected

Text Improvements

- Long tooltip lines now wrap automatically instead of running off the screen
- Unified the formatting of damage values and percentages
- Filled in missing localized text for the Thunderbolt Necklace and other items

Code Improvements

- Refactored the equipment effect framework, merging duplicated effect type implementations and cutting boilerplate
- Unified the unit convention for time-based values
- Unified how entity data and projectile data are retrieved
- Fixed an issue where expired mob effects were not fully cleaned up
